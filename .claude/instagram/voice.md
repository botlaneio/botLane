## My positions

1. Agencies should not let AI send anything to a client without a person
   approving it first. Approval is always on; there is no autopilot.
2. Account managers' time belongs on the campaigns, not on building reports
   and chasing invoices.
3. Agencies need a managed coworker that does three jobs well, not another
   dashboard or a chatbot.
4. Agencies deserve to see the bar an AI release has to clear before it
   touches client work. That is why the 12-Gate Release Standard is public.
